DATA

Turning data into evidence you can trust.

I use statistics, machine learning, and natural language processing to extract, evaluate, and communicate what data can tell us, whether it comes as numbers or as text.

My background in experimental psychology and linguistics shapes how I work with data: start from a clear question, choose methods that fit it, and report results honestly, including their uncertainty.
