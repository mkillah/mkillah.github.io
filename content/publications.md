## Journal articles

### The effect of reverse perspective and player's rating on flexible thinking in chess

Antolčić, M. & Valerjev, P. (2019). The effect of reverse perspective and player's rating on flexible thinking in chess. *Suvremena Psihologija, 22*(1), 53–65. https://doi.org/10.21465/2019-SP-221-04

**Description**

This study examined whether reversing the perspective of a chess position helps players overcome the Einstellung effect, a form of mental rigidity in problem-solving. Reversing the perspective did not reduce the effect, while higher player ratings were associated with a greater likelihood of finding optimal solutions.

## Conference proceedings

### Do higher-level chess players rely on heuristics in general as well as in chess problem solving?

Antolčić, M. & Valerjev, P. (2022). Do higher-level chess players rely on heuristics in general as well as in chess problem solving? In *Proceedings of the XXVIII Scientific Conference Empirical Studies in Psychology*. Institute of Psychology, Laboratory for Experimental Psychology, Faculty of Philosophy, University of Belgrade.

**Description**

This study compared the use of intuitive and heuristic strategies in chess problem-solving and tasks outside the chess domain. The findings suggested that chess expertise was associated with greater efficiency in chess-specific tasks but did not necessarily generalize to other domains. Better performance on chess problems was also associated with lower accuracy on general tasks.
