THE IDEA

Research players can use.

“Chess is the Drosophila of the human mind.”

Psychologists have studied chess for decades because it makes expertise visible and measurable. My own research looks at how strong players recognise patterns, and why that same skill can sometimes lead them astray.

NeuralGambit brings these findings out of journals and into practice. Its mission is to investigate the human mind using chess as a method, and to give players training grounded in cognitive science rather than guesswork.

♟ Training

Chess exercises

Training modes designed by a psychologist to show how your mind solves problems, not just whether you found the move.

  • Chess Puzzler: puzzles filtered by tags, with optional time limits
  • Memory Reconstruction: study a position for 30–90 seconds, then rebuild it from memory

↗ Data & statistics

Rating predictor

Enter your rating, age, title, and years active to see a projected improvement path, modelled on data from more than 500,000 FIDE-rated players.

  • 15-year rating trajectory with an 80% prediction interval
  • Plateau probability and rating ceiling analysis
  • Likelihood of being a fast improver
Predict a rating ↗
◎ Pattern recognition

Perception

Paste a position and a logic engine finds the checkmate patterns and positional motifs in it, such as the bishop pair, discovered attacks, or a bad bishop.

  • Imports any position in FEN notation
  • Highlights each motif on the board with an explanation
